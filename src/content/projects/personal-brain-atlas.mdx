## What it does

- **Surface, Inflate, Section, Atlas.** Each page puts the brain in a different state, using the operations neuroimagers use: the folded pial surface, the inflated surface, an axial cut, and the Desikan–Killiany atlas.
- **Section mode** cuts the surface with a moving plane. The cut face shows the cortical ribbon (between the pial and white surfaces) and the white matter inside, both taken from the reconstruction. No MRI slices are shown.
- **The HUD** is ordinary HTML: layer list, a live RAS coordinate readout, orientation letters, a 10 mm scale bar, and a methods tag.

## How it's built

A build script converts the FreeSurfer surfaces, resampled to a 40,962-vertex icosahedral mesh per hemisphere, into one glTF file per hemisphere. The pial surface is the base mesh; the inflated and white surfaces are morph targets that share its vertex order. Positions are quantized to 14 bits and compressed with meshopt, which brings both hemispheres to about 1.4 MB. See [methods](/methods) for the full pipeline and privacy choices.
