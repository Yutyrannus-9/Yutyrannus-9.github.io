## What it does

- A 12-week curriculum and codebase covering EEG simulation, filtering, and decoding with band-power features, common spatial patterns, and Riemannian classifiers.
- A reproducible pipeline on the MOABB framework that decodes motor-imagery EEG from public datasets and measures the accuracy drop from within-session to leave-one-subject-out evaluation, quantifying cross-subject domain shift.
- Compares classical pipelines (CSP + LDA; Riemannian covariance on the SPD manifold) with a deep model (EEGNet) using Euclidean Alignment, reproducing Eder et al. (2024): the deep nets showed no significant cross-subject advantage over Riemannian methods.
- An automated test suite and continuous integration keep development reproducible.
