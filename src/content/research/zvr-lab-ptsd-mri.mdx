## What I did

- Built a structural MRI analysis pipeline for 101 participants using FreeSurfer and Python, including manual subcortical quality control, covariate-adjusted ANCOVA, FDR correction, and interaction analyses.
- Evaluated exploratory L2 logistic regression with nested, repeated, and leave-one-out cross-validation and permutation testing, and added covariate residualization to check for demographic confounds.
- Analyzed trauma characteristics and learning outcomes with Fisher's exact tests, logistic regression, and Cramér's V, and automated the results export in a reproducible notebook.
- Reviewed the PTSD neuroimaging machine-learning literature against ENIGMA-PGC findings and prepared a research poster.
