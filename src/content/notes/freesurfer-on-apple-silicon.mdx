FreeSurfer 7.4.1 ships an x86 build, which runs on an Apple-silicon Mac under Rosetta. Most of `recon-all` worked as is. The TensorFlow-based tools, such as SynthSeg and SynthStrip, did not run until I exposed AVX to Rosetta:

```bash
export ROSETTA_ADVERTISE_AVX=1
```
