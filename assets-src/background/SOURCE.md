# Background image source

- File: `GFP_Neurons.png` (2317 × 1992, 6.3 MB), downloaded 2026-09-30
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GFP_Neurons.png
- Author: ManuelSchottdorf (own work)
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Description: rat cortical neurons grown in vitro, transfected to express GFP, imaged on a Zeiss Axiovert with GFP filter settings.

`scripts/build-background.mjs` crops, converts to grayscale, and recolors this image to the site palette,
producing `public/sky.avif` and `public/sky.webp`. Those adapted files are shared under the same license
(CC BY-SA 4.0), as ShareAlike requires. The credit on the site says so.
